Product & Safety Disclaimer

Status: Draft — for faculty / advisor review before any launch.

Important Medical & Regulatory Notice: PureTouch is a student laboratory formulation. It is not an approved pharmaceutical drug and has not been evaluated by CDSCO, FDA, or state licensing authorities.

1. No Efficacy or Antimicrobial Claims

In adherence to strict scientific honesty: we do not claim that PureTouch "kills 99.9% of germs", is "dermatologically tested", is "100% safe", or follows an approved official WHO formulation until comprehensive third-party lab testing has been performed.

The formulation was prepared using 75 ml ethanol, 2 ml glycerol, 4.5 ml hydrogen peroxide, and 20 ml distilled water (total measured components: 101.5 ml). The exact final alcohol percentage depends on the stock strength of raw materials and remains to be verified by an accredited testing facility.

2. General Safety & Fire Hazard Notice

Flammable Liquid: Ethanol is flammable. Keep formulation away from open fire, sparks, lit cigarettes, heaters, and high temperatures.
  • External use only: For hand sanitization only. Do not ingest, swallow, or inhale fumes directly.
  • Keep away from eyes: If contact with eyes occurs, rinse thoroughly with clean running water immediately. Seek medical help if irritation persists.
  • Keep out of reach of children: Use by minors should be supervised.
  • Storage: Keep container tightly closed in a cool, well-ventilated dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Irritation: Stop use immediately if skin redness, rash, or irritation occurs.

3. Educational Context

This project is conducted under academic guidance for educational documentation purposes only. No commercial distribution or clinical endorsement is implied.
