Privacy Policy

Status: Draft — for faculty / advisor review before any launch.

Student Project Notice: This website is an educational demonstration created as part of a college laboratory project. It is not currently backed by a commercial database or tracking service.

1. Information We Collect

By default, this website runs entirely in your browser as a static site and does not collect personal data, track browsing activity with advertising cookies, or log your IP address to a proprietary server.

Enquiry Form

If you fill out the enquiry form on the Buy Options section:

  • Current demo state: No backend endpoint is connected by default. Your inputs (name, phone number, quantity, message) remain in your local browser session and are not saved or transmitted anywhere.
  • Connected state: If the student team connects a form endpoint (such as Formspree or Google Apps Script), the information you explicitly enter will be transmitted solely to allow the team to reply to your enquiry.

Feedback Form

The feedback form collects rating numbers, category selections, and optional comments. In the demo state, no feedback is stored in a database. If connected to a backend, feedback may be reviewed to improve the project.

2. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party behavioral profiling scripts.

3. Third-Party Services

If you click on external links (such as WhatsApp wa.me or a UPI payment application link), you are subject to the privacy policies of those respective services. We do not receive payment records or banking credentials.

4. Contact for Privacy Enquiries

If you have questions about this privacy statement or the college project, please reach out via the contact channels listed on the main page (masked phone: 92702*****) or the project department supervisor.
